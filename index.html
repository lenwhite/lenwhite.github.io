In hopes of an AI plateau

September 20, 2026·
  • AI

A natural, temporary plateau in AI progress is the among the best cases for humanity.

There's one world where AI development stays a race to the bottom. Models get more powerful until we hit recursive self-improvement. A cybersecurity catastrophe — from an open model, a frontier lab, or a government lab — does critical damage to global internet infrastructure. If we survive, the world overreacts and clamps down on AI progress for a long time.

There's another world where AI development is stillborn. Amid calls for regulatory capture and fear-mongering, industry groups self-regulate or the US and China mandate it by law. The technology is strangled in its crib, gated to large corporations and governments, doled out in watered-down dribs and drabs out of fear of the four horsemen of the infocalypse.
