Voidloop helps me sleep

July 13, 2026·
  • games
  • habits

Voidloop on Steam is a simple incremental game for bed.

My criteria:

  1. Not free-to-play or gacha.
  2. Incremental.
  3. Not too engaging: repetition and progression.
  4. No background progress, timers, jams, or time gates.

You run around, swing a pickaxe and sword, use abilities, collect materials, complete quests, and earn achievements. It has the right amount of mindlessness and progression to lure me to sleep like a Minecraft lullaby.

It is also on Google Play.

Props to the developer. We’ll watch his career with great interest.

If you know similar games, let me know.
